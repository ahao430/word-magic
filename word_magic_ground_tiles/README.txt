Word Magic - Forest Ground Tiles (prototype)

Contents:
- 7 individual 32x32 PNG tiles
- Each tile is extracted from an AI-generated concept atlas and resized with nearest-neighbor sampling.
- word_magic_ground_tiles.png is a compact 4-column atlas with 2px transparent gutters.

Important limitations:
- These are prototype placeholders, not production-ready seamless terrain tiles.
- Some samples contain texture edges or inconsistent visual detail; inspect before use.
- No automatic collision shapes or Godot TileSet resource are included.
- The source concept image used a checkerboard-like background; the selected tile interiors were cropped to avoid that background as far as possible.
- Before commercial release, verify image-generation service terms and keep a project asset provenance record.
